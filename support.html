Common questions

How does scanning work?

Take a photo or tap Auto to let your iPhone find the receipt edges. TrackRabbit reads the merchant, date, tax and total, on your iPhone when it can and otherwise with a secure AI service. You check the details before saving.

What counts as a scan?

A scan counts once a receipt has been read, even if you don’t save it. The Free plan includes 15 scans a month. Adding an expense by hand never counts.

How is the category chosen?

TrackRabbit suggests a category from your own list. When you save a receipt from a merchant, it remembers your choice and uses it the next time. You can always change the category before saving.

Are the values always right?

Automatic reading is accurate but not perfect. Fields the app is unsure about are marked “Check”. Always compare important amounts with the photo. TrackRabbit is an expense tracker, not accounting or tax advice.

How do I add an expense without a receipt?

Tap Add expense on the Receipts tab, choose Manual on the camera screen, or press and hold the scan button.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone, or tap Manage on your plan in Account. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period.

How do I get a refund?

App Store purchases are refunded by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Pro but the app still shows Free.

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy, then open the paywall and tap Restore purchases. If that doesn’t help, email us.

How do I sign in?

With Sign in with Apple, using the Apple ID on your iPhone. There is no separate password. If you chose “Hide My Email”, we only see a private relay address from Apple.

Where is my data stored?

Your receipts and photos are stored privately in the European Union. Only you can see them. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Go to Account › Notifications. You can switch the weekly reminder, budget alerts and month-end wrap-up on or off separately.

Delete your account

You can delete your account and all its data at any time, directly in the app:

  1. Open TrackRabbit and go to the Account tab.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your expenses, categories, budgets and receipt photos are deleted immediately and cannot be recovered. Export your expenses first if you want to keep a copy (Account › Export expenses).

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in your iPhone’s Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

If you can no longer open the app, email info@mobirexa.com from the address linked to your account and we will delete it for you.
