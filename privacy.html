The summary above is for convenience; the full text below is what applies.

2. What we collect

Data you give us

  • Account data: you sign in with Apple. Apple shares a stable account identifier, your email address (or a private relay address if you choose to hide yours) and, on the first sign-in, your name. We also store the workspace name you choose. There are no passwords: we never see your Apple ID password. To let you disconnect TrackRabbit from your Apple ID, we keep a token from Apple that is revoked when you delete your account.
  • Receipt photos you take or upload.
  • Expense data read from receipts or entered by you: merchant, date, amounts, tax, tip, currency, line items, category, notes and the payment method label printed on the receipt (for example “Visa •• 4821”). We never ask for or store full card numbers.
  • Your settings: categories, budgets and onboarding choices.
  • Messages you send us, for example support emails.

Receipts can contain information about other people or sensitive details (for example a pharmacy purchase). Please only scan receipts you are entitled to keep, and avoid scanning documents that are not receipts.

Data created when you use TrackRabbit

  • Usage counters needed to run the Service, such as how many receipts you scanned this month (for the Free plan limit) and short-term rate limits that protect the Service from abuse.
  • Subscription status: whether you have TrackRabbit Pro, the plan, trial status and renewal or expiry date. Payments are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment card details.
  • Technical logs: our infrastructure providers record technical data such as IP address, request time and error details to operate and secure the Service. These logs are kept for a short time and are not used to profile you.

Data that stays on your iPhone

  • Face ID: if you turn on the app lock, iOS checks your face or passcode. We never receive biometric data, only a yes or no from iOS.
  • Camera and photos: TrackRabbit only accesses the camera or photo library when you choose to scan or upload a receipt, and only the photos you pick.
  • Reminders: reminder notifications are scheduled on your iPhone from your own data. Nothing is sent to a push notification server.
  • App preferences such as which onboarding screens you have seen.

What we do not do

TrackRabbit has no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking tools and no data brokers. We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

3. How receipts are read

When you scan a receipt, TrackRabbit reads the merchant, date, amounts and other details so you do not have to type them. This happens in one of two ways:

  • On your iPhone. On supported iPhones, the text is recognized with Apple’s on-device Vision and on-device language model features. The reading happens on the device.
  • With a secure AI service. When on-device reading is not available or its result fails our accuracy checks, the photo is sent from our server to Anthropic, the provider of the Claude AI models, together with your category names and recent merchant-to-category choices, so the receipt can be read and categorized. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models and is kept only for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring.

In both cases the receipt photo is uploaded to our storage so that you can see it later next to the expense. You always review the result before an expense is saved. Automated reading can make mistakes; see our Terms of Use.

Reading is automated but has no legal or similarly significant effect on you: it only fills in fields that you check and can change.

7. How long we keep data

  • Your account and expenses: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Scans you did not save: photos of receipts that were scanned but never saved as an expense are deleted automatically, usually within 48 hours.
  • When you delete your account: your expenses, categories, budgets and receipt photos are deleted immediately. Copies in encrypted backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Technical logs: kept by our providers for a short period, typically up to 90 days.
  • Support emails: kept as long as needed to help you, and no longer than two years after the conversation ends.
  • We keep data longer only where the law requires it.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you also have the right to access your personal data, to have it rectified or erased, to restrict or object to processing based on our legitimate interests, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our providers. Database access is restricted so that each account can only read its own workspace, receipt photos are stored privately and are only shown through short-lived links, and the app can be locked with Face ID. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting your data occurs, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

11. Children

TrackRabbit is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 years old to use it, or the minimum age required in your country to consent to the processing of personal data (16 in some EU countries) unless a parent or guardian has consented. If we learn that we hold data of a child without the required consent, we will delete it.
